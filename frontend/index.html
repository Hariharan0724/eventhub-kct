Targeted Problem Solved

Why EventHub?

Engineered specifically to overcome chaotic venue check-ins and unverified manual paper sheets during campus events.

✕

The Problem

"Manual attendance during college events can be time-consuming, difficult to verify and prone to duplicate or missing entries."

  • Long queues at venue desks while volunteers search printed paper lists.
  • No reliable way to prevent proxy attendance or unverified walk-ins.
  • Accidental duplicate sign-ins skewing participant counts.
  • Hours lost compiling post-event attendance spreadsheets manually.
✓

Our Solution

"EventHub connects event registration with digital attendance verification, allowing organizers to quickly identify registered participants and record attendance."

  • Instant mobile QR scanning with 1-second check-in verification.
  • Unique Registration ID with built-in duplicate check prevention.
  • Strict 4-case validation: blocks unregistered or already attended students.
  • Live organizer dashboard updating presence rates and instant CSV export.
